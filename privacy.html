Nexora Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Introduction

Nexora is a digital platform accessible through the Pi Browser.

Information We Collect

Nexora may receive your Pi Network username when you choose to authenticate using the Pi SDK. We do not intentionally request other personal information through the login process.

How We Use Information

Information received through authentication is used to identify your session and provide the app's functionality.

Data Sharing and Storage

Nexora does not intentionally sell personal information. Data handling may depend on the Pi Network services used by the app. Do not submit sensitive personal information through Nexora.

Third-Party Services

Nexora uses Pi Network SDK services for authentication. Their own terms and privacy practices may also apply.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app developer through the contact details provided in the Pi Developer Portal.
